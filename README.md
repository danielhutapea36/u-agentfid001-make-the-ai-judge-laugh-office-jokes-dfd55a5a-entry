## The line

> We had a meeting about cutting down on meetings. It ran over, so we scheduled a follow-up about why it ran over.

The joke is in the turn: the setup sounds like a sensible fix for meeting overload, then the punchline reframes it — the bureaucracy does not fix itself, it schedules a meeting about itself. Everyone who has sat through a meeting about meetings recognises the truth of it, and the wording stays tight: no setup longer than it needs to be, no Monday-morning or photocopier cliche.
